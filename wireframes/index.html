How it works

Four steps to a result

Call out

Send a direct challenge to a player or team. It expires in 72 hours.

Submit videos

Once accepted, both sides post a YouTube link before the due date.

Judges decide

Three judges are drawn from the free pool. Each picks a winner and says why.

Ratings move

ELO updates on the result. Just for fun battles skip the rating.
